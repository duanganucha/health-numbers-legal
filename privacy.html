Privacy Policy

Health Numbers Publisher authorizes only the operator's own TikTok account.

  • Data accessed: basic account identifier and permission to upload/publish the operator's own videos.
  • Data stored: an access token kept privately on the operator's computer. Nothing is shared with, sold to, or published for third parties.
  • No personal data of other people is collected, and no patient or health records are used; all statistics are aggregated public figures.
  • The operator can revoke access at any time in TikTok settings.
